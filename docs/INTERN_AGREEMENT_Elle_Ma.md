# Internship Agreement

**Effective Date:** May 11, 2026
**Internship Start Date:** May 11, 2026
**Internship End Date:** August 11, 2026, with continuation through the 2026–2027 academic year by mutual agreement

**Between:**
- **Company:** Jaden Rones, operating as UniCircle / UniCircle Connect ("Company")
- **Intern:** Helen (Elle) Ma ("Intern")

## 1. Position and Duties

The Company hereby engages the Intern in the role of **Product/Growth Intern**. The Intern's responsibilities may include, but are not limited to:

- Conducting user research and outreach to clubs, student organizations, and university contacts
- Supporting growth initiatives to drive platform sign-ups and engagement
- Building and maintaining contact trackers, outreach spreadsheets, and campaign materials
- Assisting with product testing, QA, and onboarding flow improvements
- Drafting messaging templates, social content, and community communications
- Identifying partnership opportunities with UCSD and SDSU student organizations
- Any other tasks reasonably assigned by the Company

## 2. Compensation

This internship is **unpaid**. The Intern acknowledges that the primary benefit of this engagement is hands-on startup experience, access to a real product, and a professional reference upon successful completion.

**Equity Consideration:** Upon demonstrating consistent and meaningful contribution to the Company's growth and product objectives, the Intern may be considered for an equity grant at the sole discretion of the Company. Any equity award, if offered, will be governed by a separate equity or option agreement outlining vesting schedule, cliff, and terms. Nothing in this Agreement guarantees or creates an entitlement to equity.

## 3. Time Commitment

The Intern is expected to contribute approximately **___ hours per week**. Specific hours are flexible and to be coordinated directly with the Company. The Intern is not required to work on-site and may work remotely.

## 4. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on **May 11, 2026** and runs through an initial term ending **August 11, 2026**. Upon completion of the initial term, the internship will continue into the 2026–2027 academic year by mutual agreement, with terms to be confirmed in writing before the start of the fall quarter.

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time with **three (3) days written notice** (email is sufficient). The Company may terminate immediately for cause, including breach of this Agreement or the accompanying NDA.

## 5. Relationship of the Parties

The Intern is an **independent contractor**, not an employee of the Company. The Intern is responsible for their own taxes and is not entitled to employee benefits, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or agency relationship.

## 6. Intellectual Property and Work Product

All work, materials, strategies, content, code, data, and other output created by the Intern in connection with this internship ("Work Product") shall be the sole and exclusive property of UniCircle Connect. The Intern hereby assigns all right, title, and interest — including all intellectual property rights — in any Work Product to the Company. This assignment survives termination of this Agreement.

## 7. Confidentiality

The Intern agrees to the terms of the Non-Disclosure Agreement executed concurrently with this Agreement, which is incorporated herein by reference. The Intern shall not disclose any confidential or proprietary information of the Company during or after the internship.

## 8. Conduct and Professionalism

The Intern agrees to:

- Represent UniCircle Connect professionally in all communications with clubs, students, and university contacts
- Not make commitments, promises, or representations on behalf of the Company without prior approval from Jaden Rones
- Not send any outreach messages or publish any content on behalf of UniCircle without explicit approval from Jaden Rones
- Promptly communicate any issues, conflicts, or blockers to the Company

**Content Creation:** All content created for UniCircle Connect — including but not limited to social media posts, graphics, copy, videos, and campaign materials — must be reviewed and approved by Jaden Rones before publication. The Intern may share that they are interning at UniCircle on personal accounts but may not make public announcements, product claims, or partnership announcements on personal accounts without prior written approval.

**Account Access:** If the Intern is granted access to any UniCircle social media accounts, email accounts, tools, or platforms, the Intern agrees to use those accounts solely for Company purposes and to return all credentials and relinquish all access upon request or upon termination of this Agreement.

## 9. Partnerships, Outreach, and Non-Circumvention

In the course of this internship, the Intern will develop relationships with clubs, student organizations, university staff, and other third parties on behalf of UniCircle Connect ("Company Contacts"). The Intern agrees to the following:

- All Company Contacts identified, cultivated, or engaged during the internship are business assets of the Company, regardless of whether those relationships originated from the Intern's personal network.
- The Intern shall not, during the internship or for a period of **one (1) year** after its conclusion, directly or indirectly contact, solicit, or engage any Company Contact for the purpose of promoting, building, or operating a product or service that competes with UniCircle Connect.
- The Intern shall not use Company Contacts, outreach lists, or relationship data to circumvent the Company or to derive personal benefit outside the scope of this internship.

**Data Handling:** All contact lists, outreach trackers, spreadsheets, and relationship data compiled during the internship are the exclusive property of the Company. The Intern agrees not to retain, copy, or distribute this data after the internship ends and to delete or return all such materials upon request.

## 11. No Exclusivity

This Agreement does not prevent the Intern from engaging in other employment, internships, or academic activities, provided they do not conflict with the Intern's obligations under this Agreement or the NDA.

## 12. Reference and Recommendation

Upon successful completion of the internship, the Company agrees to provide the Intern with a written letter of recommendation and/or serve as a professional reference, at the Intern's request.

## 13. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of California**, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of San Diego County, California.

## 14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the accompanying NDA, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the internship and supersedes all prior discussions. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

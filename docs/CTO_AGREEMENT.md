## 1. POSITION AND DUTIES

**1.1 Role.** The Company appoints Executive as Chief Technology Officer (CTO). Executive accepts this appointment and agrees to perform the duties set forth herein.

**1.2 Responsibilities.** Executive's primary responsibilities include:

- Owning all technical architecture, infrastructure, and engineering decisions for the UniCircle platform (unicircle.app)
- Leading and managing all engineering contributors, contractors, and third-party vendors
- Maintaining production systems (Vercel, Supabase, GitHub) with appropriate security standards
- Setting and enforcing coding standards, code review processes, and deployment practices
- Advising the CEO on technical feasibility, timelines, and resource requirements
- Contributing directly to the codebase as needed, including frontend (React/TypeScript) and backend (Supabase/Edge Functions)
- Protecting all user data in compliance with applicable privacy laws (FERPA, CCPA)
- Participating in fundraising, investor diligence, and recruiting as requested by the CEO

**1.3 Time Commitment.** Executive agrees to devote approximately 8–14 hours per week to Company duties, or as needed to fulfill the responsibilities of the role. Executive must disclose any other employment or consulting engagements that conflict with Company obligations.

**1.4 Reporting.** Executive reports directly to the CEO (Jaden Rones).

## 2. COMPENSATION — EQUITY ONLY

**2.1 No Cash Compensation.** Executive serves in this role on a fully equity-compensated basis. The Company owes no salary, hourly wages, or other cash payments to Executive unless separately agreed in writing by both parties. Executive acknowledges this arrangement and waives any claim to unpaid wages based on hours worked under this Agreement.

**2.2 Equity Grant.** In consideration for Executive's services, the Company grants Executive an equity stake subject to the following terms:

- **Grant:** Executive is granted **7%** of the Company's fully diluted equity (the "Equity") as an initial grant, subject to increase per Section 2.7
- **Instrument:** *(select one)* [ ] Common Stock  [ ] Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)  [ ] Profit Interest  [ ] To be documented in a separate Equity Grant Agreement
- **Vesting Schedule:** 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff
  - 25% of the Equity vests on the 12-month anniversary of the Effective Date
  - The remaining 75% vests in equal monthly installments over the following 36 months
  - Vesting requires Executive to remain actively engaged under this Agreement
- **Acceleration:** All unvested Equity accelerates and vests in full upon a Change of Control (acquisition, merger, or sale of substantially all assets) where Executive is not offered a substantially comparable role at the successor entity
- **Clawback:** If Executive is terminated for Cause (Section 7.2), all unvested Equity is immediately forfeited. Vested Equity is not subject to clawback except in cases of fraud or willful misconduct causing material harm to the Company
- **Dilution:** Executive's equity stake is subject to dilution on a pro-rata basis in connection with future financing rounds, option pool expansions, or other issuances approved by the Company's governing body

**2.3 Cap Table.** The Company will maintain an accurate cap table reflecting Executive's equity stake and will provide Executive a copy upon request within 10 business days.

**2.4 Tax Responsibility.** Executive is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes arising from the equity grant, including any Section 83(b) election obligations. The Company is not responsible for Executive's tax planning or filings.

**2.5 Expenses.** The Company will reimburse reasonable, pre-approved business expenses (e.g., software subscriptions, development tools, travel for company business) within 30 days of submission with receipts.

**2.7 Equity Review and Top-Up.** The parties agree to review Executive's equity stake annually on the anniversary of the Effective Date. At each review, the CEO may grant additional equity to Executive based on:

(a) Demonstrated contribution to product, growth, or fundraising outcomes
(b) Increased time commitment or scope of responsibilities
(c) Retention needs relative to market compensation benchmarks

Any top-up grant requires a signed written amendment and is subject to its own vesting schedule as agreed at the time of grant. Neither party is obligated to agree to a specific amount. The initial 7% grant is a floor — it will not be reduced except through standard pro-rata dilution on new equity issuances.

**2.8 Future Cash Compensation.** If the Company raises institutional funding or generates sufficient revenue, the parties agree to negotiate in good faith toward a market-rate cash compensation package for Executive at that time. Any such arrangement requires a written amendment to this Agreement.

## 3. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE (NDA)

**3.1 Definition of Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed to Executive by the Company, including but not limited to:

- Source code, architecture, technical documentation, and system designs
- User data, applicant data, student profiles, and club information
- Business plans, financial projections, pricing, and fundraising materials
- Partnerships, investor identities, and term sheets
- Marketing strategies, growth metrics, and product roadmaps
- API keys, credentials, database schemas, and environment variables
- Any information marked "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential

**3.2 Obligations.** Executive agrees to:

(a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence  
(b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent from the CEO  
(c) Use Confidential Information solely to perform duties under this Agreement  
(d) Promptly notify the Company of any unauthorized disclosure or breach  
(e) Apply at minimum the same security measures Executive applies to their own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care  

**3.3 Exclusions.** Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that:

(a) Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of Executive  
(b) Executive lawfully possessed before receiving it from the Company  
(c) Is independently developed by Executive without use of Confidential Information  
(d) Is required to be disclosed by law or court order (Executive must provide the Company advance written notice where legally permitted)  

**3.4 Duration.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely for trade secrets, and for three (3) years for all other Confidential Information.

**3.5 Credentials and Access.** Executive shall not share, reuse, or retain credentials (Supabase keys, GitHub tokens, Vercel access, API keys) outside of secure, company-controlled credential management. Upon termination, Executive must rotate or surrender all credentials immediately.

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

**4.1 Scope — Company Work Only.** This Section applies solely to work product, inventions, code, designs, writings, data, and other materials created by Executive: (a) using Company resources, systems, or Confidential Information; (b) during time devoted to Company duties under this Agreement; or (c) that directly relate to the Company's current or reasonably anticipated business ("Company IP"). Work created entirely outside Company time and resources that does not relate to the Company's business is explicitly excluded.

**4.2 Work for Hire.** All Company IP is a work made for hire owned exclusively by the Company to the fullest extent permitted by law.

**4.3 Assignment.** To the extent any Company IP is not a work for hire, Executive hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to such Company IP, including all patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, and other intellectual property rights therein, worldwide.

**4.4 Prior Inventions Carve-Out.** The Company claims no rights to any inventions, code, or IP that Executive created before the Effective Date. Executive lists any prior work Executive intends to retain in Exhibit A. If Exhibit A is blank, Executive represents there are no such prior inventions relevant to the Company's business.

**4.5 Unrelated Personal Projects Carve-Out.** Executive retains full ownership of any personal projects, side projects, or inventions developed entirely on Executive's own time, without use of Company resources or Confidential Information, and that do not compete with or relate to the Company's business. Executive is not required to disclose such projects unless they create a conflict of interest under Section 1.3.

**4.6 Assistance.** Executive agrees to execute any documents and take any actions reasonably requested by the Company to perfect, register, or enforce the Company's rights in Company IP, both during and after the term of this Agreement.

**4.7 No Prohibited IP.** Executive shall not incorporate into Company products any code or materials subject to open-source licenses (e.g., GPL, AGPL) that would require the Company to open-source its proprietary code without prior written approval from the CEO.

## 7. TERMINATION

**7.1 Termination Without Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement with fourteen (14) days written notice. No cause or explanation is required.

**7.2 Termination For Cause.** The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice for Cause. "Cause" means:

(a) Material breach of this Agreement not cured within seven (7) days of written notice specifying the breach  
(b) Willful misconduct, fraud, or gross negligence in connection with Company duties  
(c) Unauthorized disclosure or misuse of Confidential Information  
(d) Misappropriation of Company assets, funds, equity, or intellectual property  
(e) Conduct that a reasonable person would consider materially harmful to the Company's reputation, operations, or legal standing  

For clarity, a good-faith disagreement on technical direction or business strategy does not constitute Cause.

**7.3 Resignation.** Executive may resign at any time with fourteen (14) days written notice. Resignation is treated as Termination Without Cause for equity purposes.

**7.4 Equity Treatment on Termination.**

- **Without Cause or Resignation:** Vested equity is retained. Unvested equity is forfeited as of the termination date.
- **For Cause:** All unvested equity is immediately forfeited. Vested equity is retained unless termination is based on fraud or willful misappropriation causing material harm, in which case the Company may seek equitable relief.
- **Death or Disability:** Vested equity is retained. Unvested equity vests on a pro-rata basis through the date of incapacity.

**7.5 Transition Obligations.** Upon termination for any reason, Executive must:

(a) Transfer all Company repositories, codebases, documentation, and credentials within 48 hours  
(b) Cooperate reasonably for up to 30 days on a transition handoff (without compensation unless separately agreed)  
(c) Confirm in writing that all Confidential Information has been returned or destroyed  
(d) Relinquish all access to Company systems (GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, etc.) within 24 hours  

**7.6 Survival.** Sections 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.5, 8, and 9 survive termination of this Agreement.

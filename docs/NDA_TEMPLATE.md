# Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Effective Date:** _______________

**Between:**
- **Disclosing Party:** Jaden Rones, operating as UniCircle / UniCircle Connect ("Company")
- **Receiving Party:** Chloe Burcell ("Recipient")

---

## 1. Purpose

The Receiving Party may be given access to confidential information belonging to the Company in connection with a potential marketing collaboration or engagement. This Agreement governs the use and protection of that information.

---

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by the Company, including but not limited to:

- Business strategies, marketing plans, and campaign concepts
- User data, growth metrics, and platform analytics
- Partnership discussions, vendor relationships, and pricing
- Product roadmaps, unreleased features, and technical infrastructure
- Investor materials, financial projections, and funding details
- Any information marked "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party
- Was already known to the Receiving Party before disclosure
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information
- Is disclosed with the Company's prior written consent

---

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

1. Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
2. Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent from the Company
3. Use Confidential Information solely for evaluating or performing the marketing engagement
4. Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used to protect their own confidential information (no less than reasonable care)
5. Immediately notify the Company of any unauthorized use or disclosure

---

## 4. Term

This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and remains in force for **two (2) years**, unless terminated earlier by mutual written agreement. Obligations regarding Confidential Information disclosed during the term survive termination.

---

## 5. No License

Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights in or to the Confidential Information except as expressly stated. No intellectual property license is granted.

---

## 6. Return or Destruction

Upon request or termination, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information (including copies and notes) and, if requested, certify in writing that they have done so.

---

## 7. No Employment or Partnership

This Agreement does not create an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, or agency between the parties.

---

## 8. Remedies

The Receiving Party acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Company may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other available remedies.

---

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of California**, without regard to conflict of law principles. Disputes shall be resolved in the courts of San Diego County, California.

---

## 10. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior discussions on this subject. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

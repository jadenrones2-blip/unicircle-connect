# UniCircle Outreach Scripts

> These scripts are for reaching out to **students** and **club orgs** on behalf of UniCircle. Read the instructions carefully before sending anything. Your job is to fill in two blanks and hit send — don't rewrite the scripts.

---

## Before You Start

Every message you send needs **two things filled in**:

1. **[CLUB NAME]** — the name of the org you're messaging
2. **[TIMING LINE]** — pick one based on when they recruit:

| If they recruit in... | Use this line |
|---|---|
| Fall | "fall recruiting is coming up" |
| Spring | "spring rush is around the corner" |
| Both / year-round | "you're always recruiting" |
| Unknown | "recruiting season is coming up" |

That's it. Everything else stays the same.

---

## Student Outreach

Use this when DMing individual students on Instagram, Discord, or LinkedIn.

---

**Script — Student DM**

```
Hey [name] — I'm reaching out on behalf of UniCircle, a platform built to help UCSD students 
find clubs that actually match them and apply without hunting down Google Forms. 
It's live at unicircle.app.

Quick question: what's the most annoying part of finding and applying to clubs for you?
```

---

## Org Outreach

Use this when reaching out to club officers, presidents, or recruitment chairs.

---

### Option 1 — Email

**Subject line:** `Quick question about [CLUB NAME]'s recruiting`

**Body:**

```
Hey [name],

I'm [your name] — I'm working with Jaden, a UCSD student who's building UniCircle 
(unicircle.app) as a recruiting tool for campus clubs.

With [TIMING LINE], I wanted to reach out to [CLUB NAME] and ask honestly — 
what does your current recruiting process actually look like? Like where does it 
get messy or slow you down?

We're still figuring out exactly what to build and we'd rather talk to clubs 
directly than guess. If you have 15 minutes to just tell us what's broken, 
that'd genuinely help us more than anything.

No pitch, just a conversation.

— [Your name]
jrones@ucsd.edu | unicircle.app
```

---

### Option 2 — Instagram / LinkedIn DM

```
Hey [name]! I'm [your name], working with a UCSD student who's building a recruiting 
tool for campus clubs called UniCircle. With [TIMING LINE] I wanted to ask — 
what's the most painful part of your recruiting process right now? 

We're early and trying to build the right thing. Honest answer from [CLUB NAME] 
would actually be super helpful.
```

---

### Option 3 — In Person / Tabling

> Read this a few times until it feels natural. Don't read it off your phone.

```
Hey, are you with [CLUB NAME]? I'm [your name] — I'm working with a UCSD student 
building a recruiting tool for clubs called UniCircle. We're still early and 
trying to figure out what to actually build, so we've been talking to clubs 
directly. What does your recruiting process look like right now — like where 
does it usually break down?
```

---

## After They Respond

If they're interested → **book a 15-minute call with Jaden.** Don't try to demo it yourself.

If they ask questions you can't answer → say *"Great question — let me connect you directly with our founder."* Then loop in Jaden.

If they say they're not recruiting right now → reply:

```
Totally makes sense — I'll follow up closer to [fall/spring] recruiting. 
Mind if I reach back out then?
```

## Tracking

Log every outreach in the Notion CRM table with:
- Club name
- Contact name + handle/email
- Channel used (email / IG DM / in person)
- Date sent
- Response (yes / no / no response)
- Follow-up date

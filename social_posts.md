Google Form. Spreadsheet. Group chat.

Sometimes a shared doc. Sometimes a Notion page that nobody updates after week one. Always some version of chaos that the outgoing officers somehow survive, then hand off to the next class — who start over from nothing.

Not a CRM (too corporate). Not Airtable (too general). Not a hiring platform (students aren't employees). Nothing built specifically for the way student organizations actually work.

UniCircle is that tool. Custom application forms tied to a visual pipeline. Multi-officer scoring and voting. Automated emails at every stage. Everything your club needs, in one place, semester after semester.

## Posting Strategy

**Core idea:** Students first, orgs second. Students using it creates the demand that pulls orgs in.

**Sequencing:**
1. Post the **carousel** (slides 1–5) — student-facing hook, addresses students directly
2. 3–4 days later: **Asset 03** (Confession) — bridges to org leaders, highest relatability
3. 3–4 days later: **Asset 02** (Villain Swap) — names the tool as the problem
4. 3–4 days later: **Asset 01** (Brainwash) — direct product tease, CTA-forward

**Best times:**
- Instagram: Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday, 11am–1pm or 7–9pm local time
- LinkedIn: Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am or 12–2pm

**Repurposing:**
- LinkedIn posts → Twitter/X threads (split each paragraph into its own tweet)
- Instagram captions → TikTok text overlays on a screen recording of unicircle.app
- "600 clubs at UCSD. You've heard of maybe 10." is a strong Reels hook line
- "Your club is out there." is a good push notification / story frame

**Jaden's personal account vs. @unicircleapp:**
- Carousel slide 4 (story/founder) and Asset 03 perform best from Jaden's personal account
- Carousel slide 1, 5, and Assets 01/02 work from either account
- Cross-post and tag each other for reach

The Problem
Broken on both sides of the market

🎓 For Students

  • No central place to discover clubs that match their interests
  • Find out about opportunities through word of mouth or Instagram
  • Miss deadlines because no one told them they existed
  • End up in clubs that don't fit — no data to go on

📋 For Club Leaders

  • Running a 200–800 person hiring process with zero infrastructure
  • Rebuild the same Google Form every single cycle
  • Scores tracked in someone's head or a shared doc
  • Great candidates lost because the process is chaotic

The Solution
UniCircle is the operating system
for student organizations
🔍

Discovery — for Students

Profile + quiz + resume upload. We surface clubs and events that match. Browse, compare side-by-side, apply — one platform.

📋

Recruitment Pipeline — for Clubs

Custom application forms, kanban pipeline, weighted rubric scoring, officer deliberation votes, automated email notifications.

👥

Team Management

Invite co-officers with a single link. Real-time shared pipeline access. Each officer gets their own scoring view.

📊

Data Layer (Moat)

We sit between students and orgs — capturing which clubs are competitive, what backgrounds get in, what correlates with retention. No one else has this.

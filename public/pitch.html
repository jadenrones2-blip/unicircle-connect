Founder Story
I transferred to UCSD
not knowing anyone.
"I started talking to the people actually running these clubs — the presidents, the recruitment chairs. I asked: how do you actually manage all of this? The answer was always the same. Google Forms feeding into a spreadsheet. A shared notes doc. A group chat where decisions got buried under messages."
These organizations run 200–300 person recruitment processes with tools that weren't built for them. That's when the idea clicked — one platform, built specifically for this, that handled everything.
The Problem
Broken on both sides

🎓 For Students

  • No centralized place to discover clubs
  • Find out about opportunities through word of mouth
  • Miss deadlines because no one told them
  • End up in clubs that don't actually fit

📋 For Club Leaders

  • Running a hiring process for a small business — with zero infrastructure
  • Rebuild the same Google Form every cycle
  • Someone loses track of an applicant every quarter
  • Great candidates slip through the cracks because the process is chaotic

The SIC Story
Why I'm standing here

Idea Validation

SIC pushed me to validate before I built too much — to talk to actual club leaders, not just assume I understood the problem. That changed what I built and saved months going in the wrong direction.

Product Direction

Through conversations, workshops, and industry connections — SIC helped me get rigorous about the hard questions. Who exactly is the customer? What do you build first? How do you know you're building the right thing?

Accountability

When you're a solo founder building from scratch, it's easy to disappear into your own head. SIC kept me moving — deadlines, check-ins, people who would actually ask the hard questions.

Hiring — Right Now

Building a team for the first time. SIC is in that room with me again — helping me think through who I need, what to look for, and how to structure early hires.
